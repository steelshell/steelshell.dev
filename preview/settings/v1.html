Steelshell · Settings redesign · v1

Split view

Settings becomes a two-pane screen, the way System Settings works on macOS and Settings on Windows 11. A 220px column holds search, your account with its sync status and seven categories, and the pane next to it shows a whole category at once. Every simple value changes in place with a segmented control, a stepper, a switch or the theme grid, so the only pages left are the ones with real depth, like agent access per server or the setup guides. The Terminal pane opens on a live preview that redraws as you pick a theme, a size or a cursor, and on the phone the same categories become an iOS-style root list that opens straight onto its controls.

Theme of the mockup

What changes for you

  • The whole window is used: 25 rows in one 720px column become 7 categories next to their settings
  • Terminal theme, font, size and cursor leave Appearance and sit in Terminal, under a live preview
  • No subpage for a value with 3 or 5 options, it changes right in the row
  • Search finds a setting by its name, its hint or its shortcut, in every category at once
  • The account shows sync and devices, and AI agents get their own category with status at a glance
