Steelshell settings redesign · v2

Overview

Settings opens as a calm overview of how this Steelshell is set up. The account sits on top with its sync state and the three devices that share it, and every category below is a tile that already shows its current value: your prompt in your terminal theme, font and cursor, the app appearance switch, whether agents are on and what they did today. Simple choices change right there with segmented controls, steppers and switches, and a category page groups the rest into cards next to a live preview.

What changes for you: no 25-row column with an empty right half and no subpage for a three-option value. Terminal settings leave Appearance and get their own page with a theme gallery you can see before you pick, AI agents move from the bottom to the first screen, and search finds any setting.
